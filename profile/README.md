### Avanobat (آوانوبت) — online doctor appointments 👋

> 🩺 **Access to a good doctor is everyone's right.**

Avanobat makes getting healthcare simpler: find a doctor, pick a time, and book — in a few clicks, any time, from anywhere.
A digital health platform built and operated by **[Vira Web Aria Co.](https://onwebs.ir)**

## 🔎 For patients

| | |
|---|---|
| **Smart search** | Filter by city, specialty and time of day (morning / midday / evening), or sort by first available appointment. |
| **Full doctor profiles** | Credentials and medical council number, office address with directions, services and patient reviews. |
| **Book in a few clicks** | Pick a slot and confirm — no phone calls, no waiting. |
| **Track your appointment** | Look up and follow the status of a booking at any time. |
| **Health library** | Guides and articles written with specialist doctors — from radiotherapy preparation and side effects to nutrition during treatment. |

**Specialties on the platform today:** radiotherapy · dentistry · general practice · orthopedic surgery · speech therapy

| | |

| 🗓️ **Easy schedule management** | A smart calendar with automatic reminders and fewer scheduling errors. |
| ⭐ **Branding for your specialty** | A professional profile, your services, and patient reviews. |
| 📊 **Reporting and analytics** | Track how the practice is performing and how your patient base is growing. |

→ [Register your practice](https://www.avanobat.ir/doctor-register) — applications are reviewed within **24–48 working hours**.

---

## ⚙️ How it works

`1` Search by city, specialty or doctor → `2` Pick a doctor and an available slot → `3` Confirm with an SMS code → `4` Track your appointment
